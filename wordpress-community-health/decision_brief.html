Decision brief

WordPress is still the default CMS, but the signals are softer.

Use WordPress as a strong default when installed reach, ecosystem depth, and open-source control matter. Treat growth and new-site momentum as softer: the report shows fewer new tracker reporters and more visible builder pressure.

Decision readout: still healthy, with slower entry.

WordPress remains dominant on installed-share evidence and has broad ecosystem activity. The work queue is closer to balanced than the backlog size alone suggests. The softer areas are new participant entry, older backlog, and incomplete direct evidence for new-site share, search interest, and broad job demand.

Evidence strength

Strong: installed share, ticket flow, backlog, release/community activity.

Useful proxy: BuiltWith pipeline, HTTP Archive tracked share, Stack Overflow, HN hiring, Jobs board.

Still partial: true new-site cohorts, Google Trends, broad labor-market exports, long support history.
